Solar energy for a community library

The library installed solar panels on its south-facing roof. The panels convert sunlight into electricity for lighting, computers and ventilation. A battery stores surplus production during the afternoon and supplies the building after sunset.

Before installation, the team measured electricity use every hour for a month. This helped them choose a battery that matched evening demand. They also improved insulation and replaced old lamps with efficient LEDs. Reducing consumption made the renewable supply more useful.

The monitoring display compares power generation with consumption. Cloud cover lowers production, while clean panels and unobstructed sunlight improve it. Staff inspect the installation regularly and publish monthly energy totals.
