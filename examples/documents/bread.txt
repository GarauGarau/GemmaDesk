Making a loaf of bread

Mix flour, water, yeast and salt into a soft dough. Knead until the dough becomes smooth and elastic, then leave it covered in a warm place. During fermentation, yeast produces gas that makes the dough rise.

Shape the loaf gently after the first rise. Let it rest again while the oven heats. A little steam at the start of baking helps the crust expand before it becomes firm. Bake until the loaf is golden and sounds hollow when tapped underneath.

Allow the bread to cool before slicing. Cooling gives the interior time to set and prevents a sticky texture. A longer fermentation can develop a richer flavor without adding more ingredients.
