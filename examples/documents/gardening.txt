Growing vegetables in raised beds

Raised beds make it easier to manage soil, drainage and paths in a small garden. Fill them with soil and mature compost. Choose vegetables suited to the season and leave enough room for their roots and leaves to grow.

Water near the roots in the early morning. Mulch helps the ground retain moisture and reduces the growth of weeds. Check the soil before watering, because too much water can harm roots as easily as drought.

Rotate crops between beds from year to year. Beans, leafy vegetables and root crops have different needs. A notebook recording planting dates, rainfall and harvests helps improve the plan for the following season.
